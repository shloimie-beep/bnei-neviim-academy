FAQ

Is this the final public One Time launch?

No. This is a shared review deployment packet for PR #5. Final launch still needs operator review, deploy/live-smoke proof, and approval for any live workflows.

Can this page charge a card or send an email?

No. Payment, email, WhatsApp, Zoom, Vimeo upload, DNS, external CRM writes, and access automation are blocked until explicitly approved.

Why is the promo video not embedded?

The video source was traced, but the shared review avoids unapproved child/crowd imagery. Safe stage-only stills and the approved portrait are used instead.

Use the operator packet under `ops/one-time-mishnah/operator-ui-review/` and the linked review routes above.
