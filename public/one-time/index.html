Meet Rabbi Eli Scheller

A storyteller your son will remember.

Rabbi Eli Scheller isn’t just another teacher. He’s one of the most sought-after storytellers and speakers in the Jewish world — known for his humor, warmth, clarity, and ability to make Torah unforgettable.

His shiurim have reached audiences through TorahAnytime, 24Six, Loop, NakiRadio, Mishpacha Magazine, and live events across the world.

Now, through One Time Mishnayos, your son can learn directly from Rabbi Scheller every day — with excitement, accountability, and geshmak.
