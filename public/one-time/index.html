FAQ

Quick answers before you start.

When does the program start?

The new cycle starts from the beginning on Rosh Chodesh Elul.

Is it live or recorded?

The core experience is the live class. Review and follow-up materials help students keep learning between shiurim.

Can parents track attendance?

The immediate free-class signup captures your contact details for follow-up. Parent tracking is a later member experience and is not opened by this form.

Can students contact the rabbi?

Questions can be routed through the safe class follow-up process. A student portal account is not opened by the public signup.

How long does it take to finish Mishnayos?

The program is built to complete Mishnayos in about two and a half years.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Families can start with 30 days free.
