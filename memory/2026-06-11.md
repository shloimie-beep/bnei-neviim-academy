## Content Library v2 Audit And Build Brief

Raw operator audit, distilled:

- The checked repo is the correct `shloimie-beep/bnei-neviim-academy` system,
  not the earlier Lovable assessment app.
- The current BNA Operations / Content system already has ingestion,
  transcript storage, parsed material, generated outputs, versioned prompts,
  approved examples, and weekly bundles.
- The gap is that it is still mostly a content repurposing/draft pipeline, not
  a true knowledge library.
- Existing tags and filters are too shallow: content jobs and class sessions
  keep much of their semantic information in `transcript_text`, `transcript_json`,
  `parse_json`, and flexible class-session JSON arrays.
- Operations topic chips are still partly inferred by display-side regex rules,
  which can misclassify Torah, nutrition, coding/system tasks, and real class
  topics.
- The desired system is normalized and source-aware:
  `Content Job -> Segments -> Ideas/Punchlines -> Claims -> Sources -> Research
  Tasks -> Tags/Taxonomy -> Repurposing Assets`.
- Required database additions: taxonomy terms, job terms, segments, claims,
  sources, claim-source joins, and research tasks.
- Required parser addition: keep mixed-recording parser v1 for routing tasks,
  students, Torah progress, and class notes; add `content-library-parser-v2`
  for segments, claims, topics, sources, research tasks, source status, and
  repurposing angles.
- Required filters: query, status, content type, domain, topic slug, Torah area,
  masechta, parsha, halacha area, science domain, source status, research
  status, repurposing status, audience, project, dates, transcript presence,
  source presence, and review need.
- Required Operations queues: Needs Torah Source, Needs Sefaria Sheet, Needs
  Scientific Source, Needs Human Review, Ready to Repurpose, Ready to Publish,
  and Published.
