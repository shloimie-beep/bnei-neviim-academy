# Component Patterns

- Top announcement bar: full-width yellow strip with bold launch copy and a
  small right-side deadline note.
- Header: sticky dark toolbar, logo left, nav right, yellow Start Free CTA,
  canonical `/rabbi-member` login link.
- Hero: full-width media placeholder, dark overlay, centered serif headline,
  yellow and white CTA pair, trial microcopy, and bottom yellow ticker.
- Press strip: light background, centered heading, logo row with responsive
  wrapping and Naki placeholder.
- Program cards: dark cards with media placeholders and concise benefit copy.
- Funnel/feature sections: alternating light/dark bands, scannable bullets,
  no private Operations text.
- Final CTA: dark/yellow section with existing first-party interest form, no
  checkout, no send, no access grant.
