# Design Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Near black | `#080910` | Page background, header, hero overlay |
| Dark panel | `#12151d` / `#171b24` | Dark cards and outcome panel |
| Bright yellow | `#ede518` | Announcement bar, CTA, ticker, bullets |
| White | `#ffffff` | Hero text and secondary CTA |
| Light paper | `#f7f7f1` | Press, FAQ, and funnel contrast bands |
| Serif display | Georgia fallback | Hero and section headlines |
| Sans body | Inter/system fallback | Nav, body, forms, buttons |

Spacing should stay generous. Cards keep 8px radius or less with subtle
borders. Buttons are high contrast, slightly rounded, and visually consistent.
