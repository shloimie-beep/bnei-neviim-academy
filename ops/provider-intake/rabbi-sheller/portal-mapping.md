# Portal Mapping

BNA school parent/student portal:

- Parent dashboard.
- Student dashboard.
- Private meeting schedule.
- Weekly update/video/newsletter.
- Goals/accountability where parent-visible.
- Assignments.
- Questions.
- Documents.
- Calendar.
- Messages/help.
- Parent Help Assistant and Student Helper.

Rabbi/provider participant portal:

- Program/class info.
- Next class/schedule.
- Zoom/class links later.
- Worksheets/source sheets.
- Questions/posts.
- Messages/help.
- Payment/access status.
- Optional simple provider helper.

Do not show BNA goals, BNA check-ins, BNA private accountability, or Google Classroom accountability in the provider participant portal unless explicitly enabled later.
