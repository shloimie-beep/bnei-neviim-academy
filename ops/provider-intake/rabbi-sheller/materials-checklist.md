# Materials Checklist

Collect non-secret materials in a Drive folder or repo folder:

- Current class/program names and descriptions.
- Membership tiers and pricing.
- Video/library structure.
- Vimeo/Replit/app URLs that members use.
- Worksheets/source sheets.
- Class recordings and transcript samples.
- Question/comment/thread examples.
- Reminder messages and welcome messages.
- Refund/cancellation/access policy.
- Past member/customer list export.
- Testimonials/quotes with permission status.
- Any existing brand or landing-page copy.

Mark every item:

- `received`
- `missing`
- `needs Rabbi review`
- `secret - do not store in Git`
- `ready for audit`
