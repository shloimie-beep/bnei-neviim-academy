End of Week Update - Parshas Korach
כ"ו בסיון תשפ"ו | June 11, 2026

Quick summary of the video:
- This was a strong week of real growth. The boys are becoming more comfortable with each other, and naturally that means more buttons were pushed and real emotions came up.
- We are using those moments as education, not as frustration.
- A big focus now is learning how to communicate and have fun without raised voices, crossed boundaries, or someone getting hurt.
- The goal is not just to tell a boy "speak nicely." The goal is to help him see that chesed, kavod, and caring about another person's space and feelings are values he already wants to live with.
- When a boy can notice, "My action did not match what I really value," then the correction becomes real learning.
- This coming week we will keep working on communication, chesed, and kavod in the group.
- We will also be starting short individual check-ins with each boy between 9:00 and 10:00 before learning. More details and app logins are coming soon.
- Planning for next year is also moving forward, with the new year beginning July 1st. More information will come by email.

What we learned this week:
- We continued learning about Yehoshua and the beginning of Klal Yisrael entering and conquering Eretz Yisrael. That brought up the question: if the Torah is here to teach us what to do, why does it begin with Bereishis and creation? We learned Rashi's answer: Hashem first shows that the whole world belongs to Him, and He gives Eretz Yisrael to whom He chooses.
  "כח מעשיו הגיד לעמו לתת להם נחלת גוים."
- From there, the boys asked about the wars of Yehoshua, the spoils after battle, the giants, and how the Jewish people knew that Hashem was really bringing them into the land.
- We reviewed Moshe Rabbeinu, Aharon, Miriam's well, the mann, and the forty years in the Midbar. One of the great questions was whether the mann could taste like something not kosher, since it tasted like whatever a person wanted.
- We learned about serving Hashem with joy and using the physical world in a holy way. That led to the question of fasting on Shabbos and Yom Tov: is closeness to Hashem supposed to come through pushing away pleasure, or through using Hashem's world the right way?
- We learned the sugya around Adam naming the animals and then naming Chava, and how Rashi helps us understand the order of the pesukim. That opened up a discussion about Lashon HaKodesh, Aramaic, Onkelos, and why some Torah ideas are revealed clearly while others stay hidden.
- We began Orchos Tzadikim, the pathways of the righteous, with a focus on respect, leadership, and self-control: a real leader is not the person who demands honor, but the person who serves others and becomes needed by the group.
- We also discussed Reuven and Gad wanting land on the other side of the Yarden because of their sheep. The boys asked: why would someone choose good grazing land over entering Eretz Yisrael? That led into responsibility, fighting together with Klal Yisrael, and what it means to put the group before your own comfort.
- This connects beautifully to Parshas Korach. The boys are learning that disagreement, frustration, and strong feelings are not the problem. The question is whether we turn those moments into growth, respect, and responsibility, instead of machlokes.
  "ולא יהיה כקרח וכעדתו."

Kol hakavod to all the boys for showing up, learning, asking real questions, and working on themselves. This is real growth.

Have a good Shabbos!
