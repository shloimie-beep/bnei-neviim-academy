# Parent Messaging


Status: draft seed


## WhatsApp Updates


- Short enough to read quickly.
- Concrete bullet points are preferred.
- For video captions, summarize the video points first, then add a compact
  "This week at BNA" recap if there are class/week details.
- When a video caption is also covering the weekly learning recap, put the date
  at the top, then use this order:
  - first section: only the uploaded video's key points as short bullets
  - second section: "What we learned this week" with the actual topics,
    sources, discussions, and questions from the week
  - final section: the learner/Masmid shout-out, when one was mentioned
- Use emojis in the first video bullet section when Shloimie explicitly asks
  for them; otherwise keep the copy plain.
- Use Shloimie's direct parent-update tone: professional, practical, and
  concise. Avoid fluffy praise and generic inspiration.
- Include logistics clearly when relevant: food, location, timing, tomorrow's plan.
- Preserve the feeling of the day without inventing details.
- Write Hebrew and Torah terms in Hebrew letters when Shloimie gives the Hebrew
  term, even if Telegram transcription spells it phonetically. Common examples:
  `גאווה`, `אורחות צדיקים`, `פרשת שלח`, `פרק רביעי`, `רביעי`, `מתמיד`,
  `מתמיד השבוע`, and `מתמיד הישיבה`.


## Email Updates


- Use for details that need permanence or clarity.
- Keep the tone personal and calm.
- Do not make parents dig for next steps.
