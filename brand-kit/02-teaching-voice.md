# Teaching Voice


Status: draft seed


## Voice


- Warm, human, and direct.
- Serious about Torah and growth without sounding institutional.
- Specific enough that parents can feel what happened that day.
- Avoid over-polished marketing language when a simple honest update is stronger.
- When discussing routines such as sleep, breakfast, food, or screens, write in
  a professional and practical way: how the routine affects attention,
  regulation, focus, and Torah learning.
