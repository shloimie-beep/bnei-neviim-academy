# Student Growth Principles


Status: draft seed


Track student growth through:


- Questions they asked.
- Goals they accepted.
- Decisions made in class or private meetings.
- Repeated patterns that need support.
- Strengths and interests that help us teach the child better.
- Self-knowledge: what helps the boy focus, regulate, recover, and choose well.
- Meaningful responsibility: roles, work, or commitments that connect to
  autonomy, mastery, and purpose rather than compliance alone.
