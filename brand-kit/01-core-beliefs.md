# Core Beliefs


Status: draft seed


These are durable BNA principles. Add only ideas that should guide many future pieces of communication, not one-time class details.


## Principles


- Torah learning should meet the whole child: mind, heart, middos, curiosity, confidence, and responsibility.
- Parent communication should be warm, honest, grounded, and practical.
- Student growth should be tracked through real moments: questions asked, goals chosen, private check-ins, and decisions made.
- The current BNA model is home-based integrative Torah learning, not a
  traditional school and not the archived family-accountability app.
- Autonomy means structured freedom with coaching, clear roles, honest feedback,
  and responsibility; it does not mean leaving boys without expectations.
- Practical real-world skills should support Torah learning and whole-child
  growth, not replace the Torah-centered model.
