# Phrases To Avoid


Status: draft seed


- Avoid vague hype.
- Avoid sounding like a generic school brochure.
- Avoid public claims that were not directly supported by the recording/transcript.
- Avoid "the practical message is simple" and similar filler.
- Avoid "that is very special" unless the operator explicitly uses that line.
- Avoid "if Torah really matters, the basics have to support it"; say the
  practical routine point directly.
- Avoid meta labels like "Main message from this week's video" when the copy is
  intended to sit underneath the video.
