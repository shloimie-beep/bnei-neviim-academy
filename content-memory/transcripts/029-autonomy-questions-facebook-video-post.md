One of the clearest signs that a child is really learning is the kind of questions he starts asking.

This week the boys were asking questions that go straight to autonomy and free choice:

If a non-Jew can become a Jew, why can't a Jew become a non-Jew?

When Hashem shows open miracles, does a person have more free choice or less?

If the mann could taste like anything, could someone choose a taste that is not kosher?

If someone does many good things but carries pride inside, what does that do to who he is?

These are not worksheet questions. They are the questions of boys trying to understand what it means to have a neshama, a will, responsibility, and a real relationship with Hashem.

At Bnei Neviim, we want Torah learning to become something a child owns from the inside. Curiosity first. Conversation first. Autonomy with accountability.
