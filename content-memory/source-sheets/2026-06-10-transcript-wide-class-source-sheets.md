## Sheet 1 - Food, Body Balance, Sleep, And Torah Focus

Transcript coverage: #2, #4, #8, #9, #18, #19, #20, #21, #26.

Who talks about it: Shloimie frames food, sleep, exercise, and focus as avodat
Hashem issues; student goal discussions include Menachem Mendel, Eitan Chaim,
Amitai, Huda, and Hillel.

Class questions/topics:

- How should boys relate to food, sugar, exercise, sleep, and Torah focus?
- Is bodily health just "secular health," or part of avodat Hashem?
- How do pleasure and discipline fit with Torah sweetness?

### Source 1.1 - Rambam: All Actions Directed To Knowing Hashem

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 3:2.
- Hebrew excerpt: "שיכוין לבו וכל מעשיו... לידע את השם".
- Explanation: Eating, sleeping, business, and bodily care are not neutral when
  intentionally directed toward serving Hashem.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Human_Dispositions.3.2>
- Links to: Shloimie's food/sleep/exercise framing in #2, #18, #19, #21.

### Source 1.2 - Rambam: Health Is Part Of The Ways Of Hashem

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 4:1.
- Hebrew excerpt: "היות הגוף בריא ושלם מדרכי השם הוא".
- Explanation: The body must be cared for because sickness blocks a person from
  knowing and serving Hashem properly.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Human_Dispositions.4.1>
- Links to: the boys' protein, workout, no-sugar, and sleep goals.

### Source 1.3 - Rambam: Do Not Reject All Worldly Benefit

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 3:1.
- Hebrew excerpt: "שמא יאמר אדם... אפרוש מן העולם".
- Explanation: Rambam rejects extreme asceticism as the standard path; the
  class discussion can distinguish discipline from rejecting Hashem's world.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Human_Dispositions.3.1>
- Links to: #26's debate about worldly pleasure and serving Hashem with joy.

### Source 1.4 - Torah Requires A Life Not Ruled By Taavah

- Citation: Shulchan Arukh, Yoreh De'ah 246:21.
- Hebrew excerpt: "אין דברי תורה מתקיימין... במי שמתעדן באכילה ושתיה".
- Explanation: Torah does not settle in a person who lets eating and drinking
  run his life. This supports the class's anti-junk-food focus without turning
  it into a blanket anti-pleasure message.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Shulchan_Arukh,_Yoreh_De'ah.246.21>
- Links to: no-candy/no-sugar goals and Torah focus.

### Source 1.5 - Torah Can Be Chosen Even With Simplicity

- Citation: Pirkei Avot 6:4.
- Hebrew excerpt: "פַּת בְּמֶלַח תֹּאכַל... וּבַתּוֹרָה אַתָּה עָמֵל".
- Explanation: Avot describes a willingness to choose Torah even with simple
  physical conditions; for boys, the educational use should be aspiration and
  resilience, not deprivation.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Pirkei_Avot.6.4>
- Links to: the "what is sweeter, Torah or shawarma?" discussion in #4.

Open points for Shloimie/rav review:

- Which food/sleep/exercise targets should be treated as student coaching
  rather than halachic instruction?
- How to present discipline without making boys feel Torah equals deprivation?

## Sheet 3 - Gaava, Humility, Tzaddikim, And The Yetzer Hara

Transcript coverage: #4, #8, #18, #19, #25.

Who talks about it: Shloimie with Eitan Chaim, Huda, Hillel, Amitai, and other
boys. Amitai's "tzaddik with gaava" question is already expanded in the June 9
captured-question sheet.

Class questions/topics:

- Why is gaava so severe?
- How could Moshe write that he was humble?
- Can someone be a tzaddik if he still has gaava or other struggles?
- Does a more serious person have a bigger yetzer hara?

### Source 3.1 - Gaava Is Called An Abomination

- Citation: Proverbs 16:5.
- Hebrew excerpt: "תּוֹעֲבַת יְהֹוָה כׇּל־גְּבַהּ־לֵב".
- Explanation: This is the pasuk quoted in the class's Orchos Tzadikim gaava
  discussion.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.16.5>
- Links to: #8's "Toavat Hashem kol gva lev" learning.

### Source 3.2 - Moshe Was Exceedingly Humble

- Citation: Numbers 12:3.
- Hebrew excerpt: "וְהָאִישׁ מֹשֶׁה עָנָו מְאֹד".
- Explanation: The verse itself records Moshe's humility. The class question
  should be framed as a Torah Shebichtav/Ruach Hakodesh question, not as Moshe
  praising himself.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Numbers.12.3>
- Links to: #4's "how can Moshe write he is humble?" question.

### Source 3.3 - Chazal On Gaava/Gasut Haruach

- Citation: Sotah 5a:10.
- Hebrew excerpt: "כל אדם שיש בו גסות הרוח".
- Explanation: Chazal treat arrogance as spiritually destructive. Use carefully
  with boys so the point is self-awareness and avodah, not shame.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Sotah.5a.10>
- Links to: #8's "gassut haruach" language.

### Source 3.4 - Rambam Says Gaava Is Not A Middle-Path Trait

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 2:3.
- Hebrew excerpt: "ויש דעות שאסור לו לאדם לנהוג בהן בבינונית".
- Explanation: Rambam says some traits, including haughtiness, require going far
  away from the bad extreme.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Human_Dispositions.2.3>
- Links to: Orchos Tzadikim gaava work.

### Source 3.5 - Even A Tzaddik Is Not Sinless

- Citation: Ecclesiastes 7:20.
- Hebrew excerpt: "אֵין צַדִּיק בָּאָרֶץ... וְלֹא יֶחֱטָא".
- Explanation: This answers the student question by distinguishing "tzaddik"
  from a person who never struggles or never sins.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Ecclesiastes.7.20>
- Links to: Amitai's tzaddik/gaava question in #25.

Open points for Shloimie/rav review:

- If using "bigger tzaddik, bigger yetzer" in a worksheet, verify and cite the
  exact Chazal/Rishon source before presenting it as a quote.
- Decide whether to include Orchos Tzadikim excerpts directly; they were the
  class text but need a clean public-domain/source edition link before quoting.

## Sheet 4 - Miriam, Meraglim, Free Choice, And Jewish Identity

Transcript coverage: #5, #6, #8, #9, #20, #25.

Who talks about it: Shloimie with Amitai, Eitan Chaim, and the group.

Class questions/topics:

- Why didn't the spies learn from Miriam's lashon hara?
- Did Hashem "give permission" for the spies to make a mistake?
- Why did Moshe pray for Yehoshua but not Calev?
- If a non-Jew can become Jewish, why can't a Jew become non-Jewish?
- How does free choice fit with Hashem's plan?

### Source 4.1 - Miriam Was Punished For Speech About Moshe

- Citation: Numbers 12:10.
- Hebrew excerpt: "וְהִנֵּה מִרְיָם מְצֹרַעַת".
- Explanation: This is the background for why the meraglim should have learned
  from Miriam.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Numbers.12.10>
- Links to: #9's Miriam/meraglim discussion.

### Source 4.2 - Rashi Connects Miriam And The Spies

- Citation: Rashi on Numbers 13:2:1.
- Hebrew excerpt: "שֶׁלָּקְתָה עַל עִסְקֵי דִבָּה".
- Explanation: Rashi says the parshiyot are juxtaposed because Miriam was
  punished for dibah and the wicked spies did not learn from her.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Numbers.13.2.1>
- Links to: #9's central question.

### Source 4.3 - "Send For Yourself" And Permission To Err

- Citation: Numbers 13:2.
- Hebrew excerpt: "שְׁלַח־לְךָ אֲנָשִׁים".
- Explanation: The class described Hashem allowing a path that could be misused.
  Pair with Makkot 10b below for the idea that a person can be led on the path
  he chooses.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Numbers.13.2>
- Links to: #9's "Hashem gives permission/opportunity" point.

### Source 4.4 - A Person Is Led On The Way He Wants To Go

- Citation: Makkot 10b:5.
- Hebrew excerpt: "בדרך שאדם רוצה לילך מוליכין אותו".
- Explanation: This gives a Chazal frame for free choice and self-chosen paths.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Makkot.10b.5>
- Links to: #9's free-choice question and #26's Yosef/free-choice discussion.

### Source 4.5 - Moshe Renamed Hoshea As Yehoshua

- Citation: Rashi on Numbers 13:16:1.
- Hebrew excerpt: "יָהּ יוֹשִׁיעֲךָ מֵעֲצַת מְרַגְּלִים".
- Explanation: Rashi says Moshe's added yud was a prayer that Yehoshua be saved
  from the spies' counsel.
- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Numbers.13.16.1>
- Links to: #9's "why Yehoshua and not Calev?" question.

### Source 4.6 - Calev Prayed At The Graves Of The Avot

- Citation: Sotah 34b:7.

### Source 4.7 - Free Choice Is Real

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Repentance 5:1.
- Hebrew excerpt: "רשות לכל אדם נתונה".
- Explanation: Rambam states that each person has real permission to choose the

- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Repentance.5.1>

### Source 4.8 - Repeated Sin Can Close A Path Back

- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Repentance.6.3>

### Source 4.9 - A Jew Remains A Jew Even After Sin

- Citation: Sanhedrin 44a:2.

- Explanation: This is the compact Chazal source behind the answer that Jewish

- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Sanhedrin.44a.2>
- Links to: Amitai's "why can't a Jew become non-Jewish?" question.

### Source 4.10 - Convert Status Also Persists After Later Sin

- Citation: Mishneh Torah, Forbidden Intercourse 13:17.
- Hebrew excerpt: "אפילו חזר ועבד עבודה זרה הרי הוא כישראל משומד".

- Sefaria URL: <https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Forbidden_Intercourse.13.17>
- Links to: Amitai's conversion/Jewish-status question.

Open points for Shloimie/rav review:

- Make clear that these sources explain status and identity, not a casual

- If this becomes a student worksheet, separate "Jewish status" from the
  emotional question of belonging and responsibility.
