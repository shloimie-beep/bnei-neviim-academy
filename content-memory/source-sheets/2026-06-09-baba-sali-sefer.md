## Short Answer For Class

The most likely answer is **Sefer Ahavat Yisrael** (`ספר אהבת ישראל`), a
National Library of Israel record attributed to Rabbi Yisrael Abihatzira
(`רבנו ישראל אביחצירא`), the Baba Sali. The NLI title describes it as
explanations on 127 mitzvot hinted in the verse `ואהבת לרעך כמוך`, together
with derashot and chiddushei Torah.

I did **not** verify `ספר אהבת ישראל` as a Sefaria text. Sefaria is useful here
for the Torah/Rashi/class-context sources, but the sefer-name answer itself is
bibliographic and was verified through NLI catalog records.

There is also a separate NLI record for **Kuntres Yashir Yisrael**
(`קונטרס ישיר ישראל`) attributed to Rabbi Yisrael Abihatzira, with related
titles `מקור ישראל` and `באר ישראל`. If the student meant an Abuhatzeira-family
sefer rather than the Baba Sali's own sefer, clarify the names before answering.
