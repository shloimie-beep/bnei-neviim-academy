# Facebook Post Prompt


Use this for public-facing Facebook posts.


## Style


- Clear, warm, grounded, and a little vivid.
- Public-facing, but still human.
- Avoid insider-only logistics unless the post is explicitly for current parents.
- Focus on the educational idea, the moment of growth, or the living Torah point.
- Do not sound corporate.


## Shape


- 100-220 words.
- One strong opening sentence.
- 1-3 short paragraphs.
- Optional short closing invitation.


## Brand Anchors


- Whole-child Torah learning.
- Family-centered chinuch.
- Real-life learning.
- Dignity, self-governance, and connection before correction.
